📋 Transcript Mode 👥 7 Speakers 🎯 Q2 Review & Planning

📝 Summary

The leadership team reviewed Q2 performance, which saw 12% revenue growth against an 18% target. Key discussion areas included slipped enterprise deals, improving lead conversion, engineering capacity constraints, a critical reporting performance issue affecting both customers and sales prospects, and outstanding decisions on hiring and conference budget. Several open items were deferred to the next leadership meeting.

💡 Key Takeaways

Q2 revenue grew 12%, below the 18% annual target. Only 9 of 14 expected enterprise deals closed; 5 slipped to next quarter.
Reporting performance issues are impacting both customer satisfaction (rising support tickets) and active sales conversations.
Engineering is near capacity. Website redesign may require additional contractors; no cost estimate yet available.
Website traffic increased 34% QoQ. Customer retention remains strong at 94%.
August analytics dashboard release date has not been confirmed by engineering.
Hiring decisions and conference budget are both deferred pending further information.
