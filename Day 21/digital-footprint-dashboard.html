⚡ Quick Wins
🔐 Review app permissions
Go to Settings → Privacy and audit every app's camera, microphone, and contacts access right now.
🗑️ Delete inactive accounts
If you rarely use an app, delete the account — not just the app. Data lingers in servers.
📧 Use alias emails
Services like Apple Hide My Email or SimpleLogin prevent cross-service identity linking via email.
🌐 Use a VPN on public WiFi
On mobile data this matters less, but public networks expose all unencrypted traffic. A reputable VPN adds a meaningful layer.
🔑 Enable 2FA everywhere
With this many services linked to your identity, an account compromise cascades quickly. Use an authenticator app, not SMS.
