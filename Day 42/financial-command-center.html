Short, student-relevant reads and a checklist to keep you on track.

Tips for students

  • 💡 Automate a small transfer to savings the day allowance lands — before you can spend it.
  • 💡 Track subscriptions monthly; free trials are the easiest budget leak.
  • 💡 Use student discounts — software, transit, and streaming often have them.
  • 💡 Keep a small emergency buffer (even ₹100) separate from your goal savings.
  • 💡 Review spending weekly in 5 minutes rather than monthly in an hour.
